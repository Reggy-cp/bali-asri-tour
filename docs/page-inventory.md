## 1. Page Template Classification

Based on systematic inspection of the DOM hierarchy, URL schema, and content structures, the website is grouped into **6 Reusable Page Templates**:

1. **Homepage Template (`home`)**: Hero banner, TripAdvisor ratings badge, featured tour package highlights, fleet preview, reviews & gallery preview, and contact/WhatsApp booking CTA.
2. **Tour Category & Listing Template (`tour_category`)**: Category title, breadcrumbs, filterable/grid layout of tour packages with cards, durations, highlights, pricing, and inquiry buttons.
3. **Individual Tour Detail Template (`tour_detail`)**: High-res featured hero/gallery, day-by-day itinerary breakdown, price inclusions & exclusions, highlights, related tours, and floating/inline booking inquiry CTA.
4. **Private Driver & Transportation Template (`driver_transport`)**: Fleet grid (Toyota Avanza, Innova Grand, Innova Reborn, Toyota Alphard, Toyota HiAce, Isuzu Elf), vehicle specs (passenger capacity, AC, luggage), hourly/daily rates, remote area surcharge policies, and booking inquiry flow.
5. **Standard Informational Template (`informational`)**: About Us, Our Team, Charity/CSR, Bali Tour Supplier, Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy.
6. **FAQ Accordion Template (`faq`)**: Categorized questions and collapsible accordion answers covering booking, payments, pickups, vehicles, and weather.
7. **Customer Reviews & Gallery Template (`gallery`)**: Visual photo gallery and TripAdvisor testimonials.
8. **Blog Archive & Post Template (`blog_archive` / `blog_post`)**: Destination guides, travel tips, and articles.
